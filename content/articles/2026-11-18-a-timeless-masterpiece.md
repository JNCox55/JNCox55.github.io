# Intro

This game literally shaped my childhood. The first console that we had as a family in our household was the Super Nintendo. When I played the first game (Donkey Kong Country) I was younger and had a hard time with some of the levels and bosses so I would have my Dad help me out. For DKC2, I was old enough that I beat the game by myself.

# Graphics

Rare was pushing the limits when it came to graphics for the Super Nintendo when they made Donkey Kong Country, and the sequel is no exception.

# Gameplay

The level design in this game is awesome and challenging. I love how the levels change from traditional left-to-right platforming to bottom-to-top. Lots of environments and enemy variations keep the game feeling fresh.

# Music

The soundtrack for this game is legendary. David Wise did an excellent job with the soundtrack and it is impressive how he was able to get it to sound despite the limitations of the hardware. I often will listen to the soundtrack to this day as I work or draw.

# Conclusion

This game is amazing. If you haven't played it, please do yourself a favor and play it!
