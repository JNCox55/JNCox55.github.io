Every kid had the ritual: pull the cartridge out, blow into it like a harmonica, jam it back in, and pray.

## The connector problem

A lot of top-loading and front-loading consoles relied on a pin connector that pressed against the cartridge edge. Over time, those pins loosened. Re-seating the cart was what actually helped, not the breath.

## Why it *felt* like it worked

1. You re-seated the cartridge (the actual fix).
2. You did it more than once (more chances to land a good connection).
3. You remembered the wins and forgot the misses.

## The moisture problem

Breath is humid. Moisture plus metal contacts adds up to corrosion over time. So the ritual probably made the problem worse in the long run.

Still. I'm going to keep doing it.
