There are games you remember fondly, and then there are games that **still work** when you go back. Chrono Trigger is the second kind.

## Pacing that respects you

No random encounters. Enemies are visible on the map, battles happen *in place*, and the story moves fast. A full playthrough takes about 20 hours.

> "The time is now. The place is everywhere." — some marketing copy I memorized at age nine

## New Game+

Chrono Trigger made New Game+ famous. Replaying with your old stats lets you fight the final boss at almost any point. Each point unlocks a different ending.

```python
endings = {"early": "joke", "mid": "bittersweet", "late": "true"}
for when, tone in endings.items():
    print(f"Beat Lavos {when}: {tone} ending")
```
